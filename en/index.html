About Us

Looking for a Nissan dealership that offers the best in-store and online shopping experience in Montreal? You’ll find it all at Spinelli – the address of choice for all automobile services. Regardless if you choose a new Nissan model, a demo or a quality pre-owned vehicle, Spinelli Nissan offers you a convenient virtual/online shopping experience that enables you to purchase a vehicle from the comfort of your home. Spinelli also offers an amazing in-store customer service experience for those who prefer to meet our team of dedicated product specialists in person.

Spinelli Nissan is minutes away from all major highways and easily accessible by autoroute 40 from Laval, Montreal or the south shore. Choose Spinelli Nissan and get easy access to one of the largest catalogues of vehicles priced to go. All our sedans, SUVs, trucks, crossovers, and hybrid/electric vehicles are in stock, so you're sure to find the model that best suits your tastes, needs, and budget. Shop with us today; your next vehicle awaits!

Are you looking for a well-priced, reliable vehicle that is recognized throughout the industry for its incredible value, impeccable craftsmanship and comes equipped with a long list of standard features? Your Spinelli Nissan dealership in Montreal offers a complete range of the most exciting models like the all-new ROGUE, the SENTRA, and the cool-looking KICKS, total head turners.

Ready to go make the move? Whether you choose the amazing Armada, the Pathfinder, the Altima, the Leaf, the Murano or the Versa, do not hesitate to request a test drive where and when you want. The choice is yours to make. Spinelli will schedule a safe road test so you can rest assured that the model you have chosen is the one that suits you best.

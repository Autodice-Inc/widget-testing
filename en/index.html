About Us

Looking for a Toyota dealership that offers the best in-store and online shopping experience in Montreal? You’ll find it all at Spinelli – the address of choice for all automobile services. Regardless of if you choose an in-stock 2026-2027 Toyota model, a demo, or a quality pre-owned vehicle, Spinelli Toyota Lachine offers you a convenient virtual/online shopping experience that enables you to purchase a vehicle from the comfort of your home. Spinelli also offers an amazing in-store customer service experience for those who prefer to meet our team of dedicated product specialists in person. Spinelli Toyota Lachine is minutes away from downtown Montreal and easily accessible by autoroute 20.

Choose Spinelli Toyota Lachine and get easy access to one of the largest catalogues of vehicles priced to go. All our sedans, SUVs, trucks, crossovers, and hybrid/electric vehicles are in stock so you're sure to find the model that best suits your tastes, your needs, and your budget. Shop with us today; your next vehicle awaits!

Are you looking for a well-priced, reliable vehicle that is recognized throughout the industry for its incredible value, impeccable craftsmanship, and comes equipped with a long list of standard features? Your Spinelli Toyota Lachine dealership in Montreal offers the complete range of the most awarded models like the Camry, Corolla, Rav4, and Prius, which conjugates impeccable quality and reliability. Ready to make the move? Whether you choose the sublime Supra, GR86 or CH-R, request a test drive where and when it's most convenient for you. Spinelli will schedule a safe road test so you can rest assured that the model you have chosen is the one that suits you best.

Customers from all over the greater metropolitan area and from all over the province have chosen Spinelli. The excellence of our brand is recognized in neighbourhoods such as Griffintown, Lachine, LaSalle, Verdun, Hampstead, Côte St-Luc, Mont-Royal, Dorval, Sunnybrooke, Dollard-des-Ormeaux, Ile Bizard, Melocheville, Châteauguay, St-Constant, St-Rémi, Mercier, Léry, Maple Grove, Beauharnois, Vaudreuil, Dorion, as well as in Laval and throughout the North Shore.

This is why the most demanding customers shop at Spinelli, the address of choice for all automotive services, in-store and online.
